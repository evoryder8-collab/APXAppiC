# Who could lift this in one go?

Each benchmark describes one fictional character in the named form or equipment. Comic profiles and story accounts supply individual feats. For role-playing game entries, each hero’s character sheet gives a Strength rank. The licensed *Marvel Super Heroes* rules let the character lift a load of that rank on a successful yellow roll. The comparison uses the top weight of that rank. [Player’s Book, printed p. 16](<https://mshgamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/03/TSR6871.Advanced.Players.Book_.pdf#page=18>)

The game defines one ton as 2,000 lb. US comics figures use that same short ton (907.18474 kg). Japanese story figures use metric tonnes (1,000 kg). The kilogram figures are unit conversions of fictional ratings. Different versions of the same character can have different abilities.

For first-edition *DC Heroes*, a character can lift their Strength rating in Attribute Points (APs). Its manual gives 15,000 US tons at 20 APs and doubles weight for each additional AP. These comparisons start at that published 20-AP value, multiply by 2 for each point above 20, convert to kilograms, and round down to one significant digit. For example, 30 APs supports a 10-megatonne comparison. The source column identifies the original printed page or character card. [First-edition manual, Attribute Points and Effect Units](https://www.scribd.com/document/1055410248/MFG201-DC-Heroes-RPG-First-Edition)
